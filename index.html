Lipid kinetics: the report describes a growth phase (0–72 h), transition phase (72–144 h) and storage phase (144–192 h). It reports interval qL values of ~0.005 g/g/h in transition and ~0.0017 g/g/h in the later storage phase; no fitted α/β pair is inserted into the live model.

Engineering use rule: Caliche report values are reference/benchmark data. They are added for comparison, traceability and future validation. The locked CarbonONE design inputs — including 0.25 g/L/h CO₂ utilization, 104 mm ID, 110 mm OD, 210 mm U-bend C-C and 12 loops — remain unchanged.

All percentages above are entered and displayed as %, not as decimal fractions — the model converts internally (e.g. 13% → 0.13) so a typo can't silently create a 99% CO₂ stream. Data basis: CarbonONE MRPL model-basis workbook ("Live Model Inputs" / "Final Design" sheets), overridden where noted by the current locked design basis (batch, 8-day, 90% working volume, 35°C target, 13% CO₂, 0.25 g/L/h utilization, 85% capture, Re≈5000, 12 loops).
