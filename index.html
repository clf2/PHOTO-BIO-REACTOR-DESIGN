Model Logic Summary

Working volumeGeometric volume × working fraction. Changing either changes liquid inventory and every volume-based calculation downstream.
CO₂ basis0.25 g/L/h utilization, 85% capture, 13 vol% CO₂ determine required inlet CO₂ and flue-gas flow.
Gas flowCurrent basis is ≈173.1 NL/min standard and ≈223.8 L/min actual at 80°C, 1 atm.
HydraulicsRe = 5000 screening target with ρ = 1020 kg/m³, μ = 1.8 mPa·s. Changing tube ID, properties, or Re alters circulation flow.
EnergyTransient heating is an ideal complete-transfer screening model only.
CycloneTangential-entry reverse-flow cyclone is preliminary; 50 µm is a reference size, not a fixed cutoff.
Gas cleaningFilter, cooler, scrubber, demister, and NOx stages are conditional on actual flue-gas composition.
ValidationPlant flue-gas analysis and vendor/equipment data are required before final fabrication or performance guarantees.
